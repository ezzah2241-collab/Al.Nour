Find Your Path in Technology

The world of Computer Science and Engineering offers many exciting career paths. Each major focuses on different technologies and skills. Explore the programs below to learn more about them.

01

Computer Science (CS)

Computer Science focuses on programming, algorithms, software development, and solving computational problems.

Main Study Areas

  • Programming
  • Data Structures
  • Algorithms
  • Database Systems
  • Software Engineering

Career Opportunities

Software Developer, Web Developer, Database Administrator, and System Analyst.

02

Computer Engineering (CE)

Computer Engineering combines computer science and electrical engineering to design and develop computer hardware and systems.

Main Study Areas

  • Digital Logic Design
  • Computer Architecture
  • Electronics
  • Embedded Systems
  • Microprocessors

Career Opportunities

Hardware Engineer, Embedded Systems Engineer, Robotics Engineer, and Network Engineer.

03

Artificial Intelligence Science (AIS)

Artificial Intelligence Science focuses on creating intelligent systems that can learn, analyze data, and make decisions.

Main Study Areas

  • Machine Learning
  • Deep Learning
  • Data Science
  • Computer Vision
  • Natural Language Processing

Career Opportunities

AI Developer, Machine Learning Engineer, Data Scientist, and AI Researcher.

04

Artificial Intelligence Engineering (AIE)

Artificial Intelligence Engineering focuses on designing and implementing intelligent technologies in real-world engineering systems.

Main Study Areas

  • Intelligent Systems
  • AI Engineering Applications
  • Robotics
  • Machine Learning
  • Automation Systems

Career Opportunities

AI Engineer, Robotics Engineer, Automation Engineer, and Intelligent Systems Developer.

05

Biomedical Informatics (BDI)

Biomedical Informatics combines technology, computing, and healthcare to manage and analyze medical information.

Main Study Areas

  • Medical Data Analysis
  • Health Information Systems
  • Medical Databases
  • Bioinformatics
  • Healthcare Technology

Career Opportunities

Biomedical Data Analyst, Health Informatics Specialist, Medical Software Developer, and Bioinformatics Specialist.
